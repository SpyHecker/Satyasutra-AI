WITNESS STATEMENT

Case No.: 142/2026
Witness: Priya Sharma
Date of Statement: 17-July-2026
Recorded by: SI Meera Joshi, Shivajinagar PS

---

I, Priya Sharma, age 29, resident of Flat 302, Sai Apartments, FC Road, Pune, hereby state the following:

On the night of 14th July 2026, I was awake until approximately 1:30 AM as I was studying for my examinations. My flat overlooks the rear alley behind the FC Road shops.

At around 12:30 AM on 15th July 2026, I heard some metallic sounds — like a lock being forced or cut — coming from the alley below. I looked out of my window and saw a dark-colored vehicle, which appeared to be a Maruti van, parked in the alley. I could not see the color clearly as the alley lighting was poor, but it looked dark grey or black to me, not white.

I saw THREE men near the vehicle, not two. One was standing near the van as a lookout, while the other two were at the rear door of one of the shops. I could not identify which shop it was from my angle. The lookout appeared to be talking on a mobile phone.

The men were at the location for approximately 30 to 40 minutes. They loaded several boxes and bags into the van and drove away at approximately 1:10 AM. The van drove towards JM Road.

I did not call the police at that time because I was not sure if it was a legitimate late-night delivery or a crime. I only learned about the burglary when I saw the police at the shops on the morning of 15th July.

IMPORTANT NOTE: I want to clarify that I am certain I saw three men, not two as reportedly stated by another witness. Also, the van appeared dark in color to me — I would not describe it as white.

I am willing to cooperate with the investigation and can identify the approximate location of the van from my window view.

Signed,
Priya Sharma
