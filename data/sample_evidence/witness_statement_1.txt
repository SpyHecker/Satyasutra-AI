WITNESS STATEMENT

Case No.: 142/2026
Witness: Sunil Patil
Date of Statement: 16-July-2026
Recorded by: SI Meera Joshi, Shivajinagar PS

---

I, Sunil Patil, age 55, owner of Patil Hardware located at 47, FC Road, Deccan, Pune, hereby state the following:

On the night of 14th July 2026, I was closing my shop at approximately 10:45 PM. As I was pulling down my front shutter, I noticed a white Maruti Eeco van parked in the rear alley between my shop and the adjacent building. The van had a partial number plate visible: MH-12 XX 7856.

I saw two men near the van. One was tall, approximately 5'10", wearing a dark blue jacket and jeans. The other was shorter, about 5'5", wearing a black hoodie. They appeared to be unloading some equipment — I could see what looked like a bag of tools and a large backpack.

I did not think much of it at the time as deliveries sometimes happen late in this area. However, I did notice that the tall man walked towards the rear of Verma Electronics (shop at 45, FC Road).

I left the area at approximately 11:00 PM and went home. I did not hear any unusual sounds during the night.

The next morning, on 15th July 2026, Rajesh Verma informed me about the break-in at around 8:45 AM. I then recalled seeing the van and the two men and immediately informed Rajesh.

I have not seen these two men before in this area. However, I do recall that Rajesh's former employee Vikram used to park a similar-looking white van near the shop on some occasions, though I am not certain it was the same vehicle.

I am willing to cooperate fully with the police investigation.

Signed,
Sunil Patil
