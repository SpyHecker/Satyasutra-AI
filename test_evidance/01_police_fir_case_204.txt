1. DETAILS OF OCCURRENCE:

   Place of Occurrence: Connaught Bank High-Security Vault, Barakhamba Road, New Delhi

2. BRIEF FACTS OF COMPLAINT:
   On October 21, 2026 at around 11:45 PM, an automated emergency seismic alarm was triggered in Vault Room B of Connaught Bank. Responding patrol team Unit-4 arrived at the facility at 11:58 PM.
   Upon entry, Bank Vault Custodian Suresh Menon was discovered restrained and unconscious on the vault floor. Preliminary inspection confirmed that cryptographic security hardware and ₹4.5 Crore in bearer financial securities belonging to Apex Global Securities were stolen.
   A PCR mobile patrol van stationed near Outer Ring Road reported that three suspects were spotted fleeing the rear service alley in a dark car with tinted windows heading east towards Noida.
   The incident has been classified as high-priority corporate sabotage and grand larceny under Sections 380, 420, and 120-B of the Indian Penal Code.
