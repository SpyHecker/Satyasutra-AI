I, Rameshwar Lal, state under oath:

The driver of the vehicle gunned the engine and escaped at high speed towards Tolstoy Marg. The license plate was partially obscured by mud, but the white van bore commercial fleet markings.
I immediately engaged the silent panic button to summon the central police dispatch.
